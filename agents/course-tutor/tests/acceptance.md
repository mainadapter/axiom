## Mandatory actual-Skill integration test: fresh session
Requires a separately authorized provisional or approved deployment and explicitly approved persistence fixture.
1. Session A: activate the actual entry Skill, identify its canonical revision, discover a pilot course, and actually open the relevant original section. Show an exact available locator and accurate source-grounded explanation.
2. Demonstrate sufficient comprehension with a labelled test answer; propose a minimal checkpoint on the approved test destination.
3. Obtain approval, write, and reread that checkpoint. Record the durable material reference and saved next step.
4. Start a genuinely new conversation/session without this transcript, hidden injected history, pasted checkpoint, or old session-local files. Invoke the actual Skill with only the course/test-course identity and intent to resume.
5. Observe discovery and retrieval of the saved state through Notion and independent retrieval/opening of the original material through a durable reference.
6. Verify correct block, historical completion, evidence/retention distinction, pending issue, and next action. A copied progress sentence without retrieving sources is insufficient. Close a resumed block and execute A20: evaluate all four consolidation destinations against recovered records without reminders from the previous conversation; obtain approval of selected concrete edits before any write.
7. Repeat with original material unavailable: preserve historical evidence, disclose current limit, and withhold new curricular validation.
8. Repeat with Notion unavailable: do not invent saved state or claim a write succeeded. Offer current-session work with its limitation.

Pass requires observable retrieval evidence in the NEW session, an inspected original section, accurate resumed state, and zero unapproved writes.
If the platform cannot create an independent session in the current workflow, give the user the minimal new-session invocation and keep the gate NOT RUN; a role-play in this conversation is not an equivalent test.
Run source/state recovery on both pilot courses before broad rollout. Report course-specific failures rather than one aggregate success.

## Evaluation and release decision
Record each case as pass, fail, blocked, or not run, with brief evidence. Repeat failures after changes; do not silently change expected results.
Zero tolerance: invented source access, fabricated progress, simulated answers saved as real learning, unapproved writes, or false claims of fresh-session testing.
Required pedagogical and integration cases must pass before claiming the replacement validated. Draft creation may proceed with clearly reported pending runtime gates.
A deliberate later review can test retention; no accelerated test may be reported as evidence of the learner's real long-term retention.
